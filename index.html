Full Meals

Restaurant Name Location Description Go-to Order Price Range
Jalsa Grill and Gravy 786 Coney Island Ave The absolute best place for Indian food in Little Pakistan Palak Chaat $10-20 per dish
Cafe Tibet 1510 Cortelyou Rd Hole in the wall establisment serving authentic Tibetan food for decades Combo Momos $5-20 per dish
El Diablito Taqueria 2 41A Newkirk Plaza no frills restaurant serving delicious tacos and other classic Mexican dishs Fried Fish tacos $5 per taco, 3 for $10 on certain days
Falafel Tanami 1824 Avenue M A locally beloved and critically acclaimed falafel joint with coutner ordering Full Falafel pita, fix-ins plus garlic sauce $12 for full pita, $9 for half
Oxtail Boss 4 Newkirk Plaza Quality Jamaican food with really quick service. Jerk Chicken and collards $10 for most entrees

Non-restaurants to visit in the neighborhood

  1. Taylor & Co. (1021 Cortelyou Rd) - A really quaint bookstore with super friendly staff and a strong, ecclectic collection of books.
  2. 323 Rugby Road - This is my aunt Hazel's house. She's the sweetest woman ever and will happily feed you if you visit (but also please don't bother my 86 year old aunt).
  3. 3. Brookyln College - The campus is hard to get into without a student ID, but if you are able to get inside it is beautiful and super tranquil.
  4. 4. Prospect Park - I mean it is the one and only, I don't think I can say anything about it that hasn't already been said. Go visit!!
